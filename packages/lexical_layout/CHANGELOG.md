# Changelog

## 1.11.0

First release. `LayoutContainerNode` (`layout-container`, with
`templateColumns`) and `LayoutItemNode` (`layout-item`), in the Lexical
playground's wire shape and verified against fixtures generated by Lexical
0.48. Both are shadow roots; the container cannot be empty, and backspace in
the first of a layout's all-empty columns removes the layout, as upstream.

`parseTemplateColumns` and `resolveTemplateColumns` read a grid template and
turn it into column widths, or `null` when the width is too narrow for the
fixed tracks and the columns should stack. `registerLayout` brings the insert
and update commands and the structural transforms of upstream's
`LayoutExtension`.
